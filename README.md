A make.fun entry for the **Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene**
bounty: one short family scene in two lines — warm, funny and heartfelt —
written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The scene

> Dad burns the pancakes every Sunday — on purpose now, he says, because
> the burnt edges are the only ones worth fighting over.
> Mum steals the best one when no one's looking, and the dog gets the one
> nobody wanted, like always.

A whole household in two lines: a dad who loses on purpose, a mum with a
secret sweet tooth, kids who fight over the burnt bits, and a dog who
always gets the last pancake. Warm enough to make a proud judge smile,
funny in a way any family recognises, and not a word wasted.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt`: UTF-8 text, two lines, under the 280-character
gate. Python standard library only, fully deterministic — running it twice
produces byte-identical output.
